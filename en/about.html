Our story

Born from a real need

Wellnest Guide grew from the frustration of not finding a place to compare wellness retreats in Spain in a clear, honest and thoughtful way. So we decided to build it ourselves.

We are a small team passionate about wellness, conscious travel and technology well applied. Our mission is to make finding the perfect retreat as easy as booking a hotel.

Why Wellnest Guide?

Spain has extraordinary richness in wellness: from the olive groves of Andalusia to the forests of Galicia, from the beaches of Formentera to the peaks of the Pyrenees, there is a retreat for every person and every moment in life. Yet finding that retreat with reliable information, real prices and honest reviews was a frustrating task.

Major travel platforms are not specialised in wellness. Search engines show generic, ad-filled results. The centers' own websites, while beautiful, don't allow comparison. There was no reference place that put wellness at the centre.

Wellnest Guide was born to fill that gap: a curated directory with smart filters, quality editorial content and a clear vision — connecting people seeking real transformation with the spaces that can offer it.

Our mission

Make it easy for anyone in Spain — or travelling to Spain — to find, compare and book the wellness retreat that best fits their needs, budget and current moment in life.

We believe wellness is not a luxury. It is a need, and it deserves the same attention, care and rigour as any other important decision in life.

How we select retreats

Not every center makes it into Wellnest Guide. We manually review each proposal before including it in the directory. Our selection criteria include:

🌿

Authenticity of approach

We prioritise centers with a coherent philosophy and established track record, not opportunistic projects riding the wellness trend.

📋

Price transparency

We require centers to communicate their rates clearly, including what is and is not included in the price.

🏡

Quality of space

Facilities must live up to their promises. We value natural settings, well-maintained infrastructure and attention to detail.

👥

Qualified team

Instructors, therapists and facilitators must have verifiable training and proven experience in their disciplines.

Our editorial content

Beyond the directory, we publish articles and guides written with rigour to help you make better decisions: how to choose your first retreat, what it really costs, what distinguishes a yoga retreat from a meditation one, what to expect if you travel alone…

All our content is original, written by people with real experience in the wellness and conscious travel sector in Spain. We don't copy, don't auto-translate, don't publish unreviewed content.

We believe quality information is the foundation of any good decision. That's why we take the time to research, verify and explain things properly.

For wellness centers

If you run a retreat center, a yoga space or a wellness programme in Spain, we'd love to hear from you. Wellnest Guide is built to be a bridge between centers committed to quality and people looking for exactly what you offer.

We work with centers of all sizes: from small rural retreats with capacity for 8 people to large wellness complexes with year-round programmes. What matters to us is authenticity, not size.

A note from the team

Wellnest Guide is a growing project. We are small, but committed. Every decision we make is guided by the question: does this make the experience better for the person looking for a retreat?

If you have suggestions, corrections or simply want to share your experience of a retreat in Spain, write to us. We read every message and deeply value feedback from our community.

Thank you for trusting Wellnest Guide for your next wellness escape.

— The Wellnest Guide team
